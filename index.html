About

I recently completed my M.Sc. in Computer Science with a specialization in Artificial Intelligence at the University of Würzburg. My work focuses on computer vision, particularly efficient image enhancement, color manipulation, image restoration, and perceptual image quality.

I previously worked as a Student Research Assistant at the Computer Vision Lab, University of Würzburg, contributing to research on lightweight and efficient vision models.

Research Interests

  • Computer Vision
  • Image Enhancement and Restoration
  • Text-guided Image Editing
  • 3D LUT-based Color Manipulation
  • Efficient Deep Learning Models
  • Perceptual Image Quality Assessment

Selected Projects

PICE: Text-guided Portrait Color Enhancement

A lightweight framework for text-guided portrait color enhancement using 3D LUT supervision. The project focuses on efficient high-resolution image enhancement, perceptual color fidelity, and practical inference speed.

Full implementation is not publicly available due to publication and institutional constraints. A public project summary will be added soon.

Image Quality Assessment Experiments

Experiments with perceptual and fidelity metrics including PSNR, SSIM, LPIPS, and color difference metrics for evaluating image enhancement methods.
