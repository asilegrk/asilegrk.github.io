I'm a Computer Vision researcher with an M.Sc. in Computer Science,from the University of Würzburg. During my Master's studies(2023-2026), I worked at the Computer Vision Lab under the supervision of Prof. Dr. Radu Timofte and Dr. Marcos V. Conde, focusing on efficient image enhancement, restoration, and text-guided visual editing.

My recent work explores lightweight neural models for portrait color enhancement using 3D LUT supervision, with an emphasis on high-resolution inference, perceptual image quality, and practical deployment.

I'm interested in building efficient and controllable visual AI systems for real-world imaging applications.

Research interests: image enhancement, image restoration, text-guided visual editing, 3D LUT-based color manipulation, efficient vision models, and perceptual image quality.

Portrait Quality Assessment for Mobile Devices

Developed and trained deep learning models for Just-Noticeable Difference score prediction in portrait images as part of the NTIRE 2024 Portrait Quality Assessment challenge. The project involved portrait analysis, face detection, feature extraction, and perceptual quality prediction.

Efficient Image Super-Resolution

Worked on improving an efficient image super-resolution model based on the SeemoRe framework, focusing on lightweight model improvements, expert-based feature merging, and high-resolution image restoration quality.

HQ Portrait Dataset Creation

Built a face detection and preprocessing pipeline to create a high-quality portrait dataset from general image collections. The project focused on automated face cropping, portrait framing, and dataset preparation for future deep learning tasks.
